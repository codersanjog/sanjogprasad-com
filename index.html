Lessons learned the hard way

I have seen what goes wrong on Amazon. That is why I know what not to do.

Ten years on the seller side means I have already made, or watched someone else make, most of the common mistakes. You don't have to repeat them.

01

Chasing sales, ignoring profit

Sales can go up while profit goes down. Many brands only look at the top line and miss it.

02

Running ads with no ACOS target

Ad spend without a clear cost target usually ends up eating the margin it was meant to protect.

03

Weak listings hiding behind paid traffic

Ads can only push traffic to a listing. If the listing does not convert, the spend is wasted.

04

Ignoring FBA fees and storage costs

These costs quietly build up and are often missing from the real profit picture.

05

No plan before launch

Going live without a pricing and ad plan means the first weeks of data, which matter a lot, get wasted.
